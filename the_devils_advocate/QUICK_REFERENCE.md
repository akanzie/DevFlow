## 🎮 Gameplay Quick Guide

### Setup Screen
1. Enter your name
2. Choose difficulty (1-10)
3. Select category (or Random)
4. Start playing!

### Game Screen
1. Read the absurd **thesis** (red box)
2. Choose an AI **counter-argument** from dropdown
3. Write your **rebuttal** (long text box)
4. Click **✓ Gửi** (Submit)
5. See your **score** and feedback

### Scoring
- **Logic** (0-100) - How logical?
- **Persuasion** (0-100) - How convincing?
- **Creativity** (0-100) - How original?
- **Relevance** (0-100) - How well responded?

**Goal**: Get ≥80 to win the round

## 🎯 How to Win

### Winning Strategy
1. ✅ Use logic keywords ("vì", "do đó", "bằng chứng")
2. ✅ Structure clearly (intro → main → conclusion)
3. ✅ Give specific examples or facts
4. ✅ Address the counter-argument directly
5. ✅ Be creative with novel angles

### Scoring Well
- 60-79: Good effort
- 80-89: Excellent!
- 90-100: Outstanding! 🏆

### Win Conditions
- **Round Win**: Score ≥ 80
- **Game Win**: Reach 1000 points total
- **Game Over**: Lose 3 rounds in a row

## 💡 Fallacy Types (Learn These!)

| Fallacy | Example | Counter |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Ad Hominem** | "You're stupid!" | Focus on ideas, not people |
| **Strawman** | "So you want X?" (you didn't say X) | Clarify what you actually said |
| **False Cause** | "A happened, then B, so A caused B" | Point out correlation ≠ causation |
| **False Dichotomy** | "Either A or B" (ignores C, D...) | Show there are more options |
| **Slippery Slope** | "If X, then disaster!" | Show missing steps |
| **Begging Question** | "Obviously X is true" (no proof) | Ask for evidence |
| **Appeal Authority** | "Experts say..." (unverified) | Ask who & verify |
| **Red Herring** | "But what about...?" (off-topic) | Stay on topic |
| **Hasty Generalization** | "One time = always" | Need more evidence |
| **Appeal Emotion** | "Think of the children!" (no logic) | Use facts, not feelings |
